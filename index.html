Privacy Policy

Villa Protokol is an app for inspecting houses, villas and pools. It is developed by Tom (private developer).

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access.
  • Data stays on your phone. Everything you enter (houses, checklists, notes, photos, voice notes, your name, company name and logo, PDF reports) is stored only on your device. The developer has no access to it. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.
  • Permissions. Camera and photos – only when you add a photo or logo. Microphone – only when you record a voice note. Notifications and alarms – for reminders and tasks you set. Files – only when you choose to save or open a backup.
  • Sharing. A report or backup leaves your phone only when you share it yourself (e.g. WhatsApp, email, print). Then the privacy policy of the app you choose applies.
  • Deleting data. You can delete houses and reports in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the phone.
  • Children. The app is meant for professional use and is not directed at children.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
